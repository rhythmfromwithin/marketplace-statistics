Summary

All four platforms support OAuth 2.0 and can be used to build:

SaaS tools for sellers
Mobile apps for buyers and sellers
Browser extensions for price comparison
ERP / accounting integrations
Analytics and BI platforms
Multi-channel management dashboards
Automated fulfillment & inventory systems
Amazon is the most powerful but most complex and soon costly. Shopify has the most mature developer ecosystem. Etsy and eBay are free and straightforward to get started with.
