Seller Business Insights
Key Seller Statistics
  • 80% of sellers identify as women
  • 1 in 4 sellers live in rural areas
  • Average age: 43 years old
  • 89% are businesses of one person
  • 97% operate their businesses from their homes
  • 59% sold their goods for the first time on Etsy
  • 70% consider their shop a business
  • 29% - their creative business is their sole occupation
  • 11% of household income comes from creative business (on average)
  • 42% have financial dependents
  • 38% use income to cover household expenses (bills, rent, food)
  • 90% source supplies domestically
  • 28% export their goods outside their home country
  • Median monthly revenue: ~$574 per seller
  • Average session duration: 9 minutes 30 seconds
  • Mobile app GMS: ~46% of total GMS
  • Personalized products: ~33% of all sales
Source: Etsy Seller Census 2024, Printful

Financial Challenges & Motivations
  • 64% cite financial challenges as prompting them to start their creative businesses
  • 1 in 5 sellers say their business has helped them offset the rising cost of living
  • 1 in 4 sellers in business for more than a year say their Etsy income is more important now than a year ago
  • 37% reported that their business faced challenges as a result of inflation and rising cost of living
  • 2 in 5 saw a rise in the cost of utilities
  • 77% want to grow their businesses
  • 53% do not want to grow so big that they would have to hire others
  • Nearly 7 in 10 started their creative business to earn money while doing something they enjoy
